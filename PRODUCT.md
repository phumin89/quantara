The owner supplies funding and delegates portfolio planning to CEO and the specialist team. Aim for evidence-supported net profit after fees, spread and slippage; compare buying, holding, reducing and selling rather than assuming inactivity is best. No model may create financial permissions, expand the execution allowlist or bypass RiskEngine. Startup and frontend updates do not change actual financial authority. Persisted round reports describe that round's outcome, separately from current account and company status.

This direction supersedes the older Sandbox-default UI descriptions below. Ship a Live-only app with persistent left navigation and real routes for Office, Account/assets and Trading. Navigation keeps one owner session and one account reader; it does not reload the document or generate a pairing code. The colored Pixel Office remains the primary homepage view, with current account/trading state beside it on wide screens. Office artwork is bundled, stationary and explicitly not evidence of working agents. Team room status comes from the owner service; saved paper reports never masquerade as Live work. Real execution stays disabled behind the existing account, limits, reconciliation and explicit owner-activation gates. Keep Sandbox AI stopped in ordinary startup and paper/backtest verification headless and opt-in.

Repeated navigation/opening must not require pairing. New/expired browsers and explicit logout still require owner verification.

## Product Purpose

Quantara runs one observable trading company: prove decisions, execution and accounting in Sandbox, then operate a separately bound and explicitly authorized Live account. Specialist agents research and propose; deterministic RiskEngine and accounting own financial state. The pixel office makes real work visible.

The owner confirmed this scope on 2026-10-01. Simulated salary funding is retained for saved-account continuity. Replay tools and unrelated experiments are outside the operating product.

## Operating Context

The main account is the forward paper company, independent of archived demo and replay results. The Windows launcher supplies a one-time 100,000-THB simulated seed and optional ongoing simulated salary allocations. Saved funding, meetings and paper execution survive restart. Eight AI stages use immutable completed-market evidence; HOLD is valid and a completed meeting does not imply a fill.

The daily workspace presents current company status, saved meeting reports, funding, holdings and receipts. Only Company today and Money/portfolio are part of the Sandbox workspace. Live selection unmounts the Sandbox workspace and uses a separate authenticated executor surface.

## Approved Live Direction

Bitkub spot is the first planned Live integration. This reuses existing public crypto data and is not a claim of superior returns. Accounts, orders, capital and evidence are separate by mode. Actual broker funds and positive owner-defined limits determine Live authority.

Live accounting, transactions, PostgreSQL persistence, owner-control boundaries, recovery/reconciliation algorithms and account-scoped advisory components exist and have deterministic tests. Real broker normalization, evidence production, baseline provisioning/rollover, credential isolation, runtime registration and owner/deployment completion remain release blockers. The transport is not a verified production broker adapter.

See `docs/project-rework.md` for acceptance gates and `docs/live-release-evidence.md` for detailed evidence. Activation and actual real-money transactions require separate explicit owner authorization.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sandbox is the default; it never supplies capital or authority to Live.
- RiskEngine is the deterministic final authority. LLM output is untrusted.
- Paper fills require fresh post-decision executable quotes and reconciled accounting.
- Crypto quotes use public Bitkub depth; equities need separately configured read-only market data.
- Eight hosted AI stages are bounded by call, token and timeout budgets. Invalid evidence/output or unavailable providers produce no new order.
- Successful role summaries and reported usage are saved; failed meetings never present rejected artifacts as completed analysis.
- Completed-bar analysis and strategy-validation evidence remain inputs to trading advice; replay simulation endpoints are removed.
- Synthetic fixtures exist only in deterministic tests and must be labeled when shown.
- No Ollama/local-AI runtime integration, withdrawals, bank transfers, leverage, margin or short selling.
- Broker credentials stay outside browser code, logs, prompts and model child processes.
- Technology: .NET 10, ASP.NET Core, SignalR, Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL and Docker Compose.
- Pixel Agents is adapted under its existing license without unrelated coding-agent runtime behavior.

## Positioning

Show real company state, decisions and consequences. Agent activity is observable work, not proof of profit or a substitute for accounting.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Quantara.
- Preserve the pixel office as the main visual model.
- Sandbox/paper and Live mode are unmistakable.
- Agent configuration or idle motion never implies a completed meeting.
- No historical result is described as a prediction or guaranteed profit.

## Evidence on Hand

Deterministic tests cover paper funding/execution, AI contracts, risk denials, restart safety and Live accounting/security boundaries. Tests and paper results do not establish working production Live execution or future profitability.

## Product Principles

Show real state. Keep financial authority deterministic. Keep one daily operating path. Save enough evidence to explain a decision after restart. Fail closed on missing configuration, stale evidence or reconciliation failure. Finish a complete operating flow before adding a new strategy, provider or asset class.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use text as well as color for state. Preserve keyboard focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, mobile layouts and clear unavailable states without invented zero balances.
