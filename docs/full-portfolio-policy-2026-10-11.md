# Full reconciled portfolio policy

The setup screen showed the initial authorized baht amount three times. That amount was persisted funding authority, not a hard-coded 5,000-THB default. However, a fixed grant did not match the owner's instruction to delegate the full portfolio and reuse verified trading gains. The daily turnover ceiling also restricted capital to approximately one cycle, including both the entry and exit legs.

An explicit owner-only `UseFullPortfolio` grant now derives buying power and the per-order ceiling from current reconciled portfolio equity. Incurred daily losses remain recorded; the maximum daily loss includes both incurred loss and remaining equity, allowing the owner-delegated full-risk policy without resetting performance. Cash fees and reservations still consume available money. Sells remain restricted to verified owned, unreserved quantity, including at prices above initial funding. The full policy permits settled capital to be recycled instead of imposing the initial daily turnover amount. Fixed grants retain all their previous limits.

Research packets carry the explicit policy and effective current capital to the company. The model receives concise instructions describing capital reuse. Plan validation, reservations and dispatch still use independent deterministic executor validation. Models cannot set this grant, change the mandate, enlarge the symbol allowlist, clear Stop, activate accounts or replace broker evidence. No borrowing, leverage, withdrawals or external transfer implementation was added. Unexplained deposits and withdrawals continue to fail reconciliation; they are not absorbed as profit or silently allocated. The original 30-THB manual test is not a performance result.

The new field defaults to false for legacy stored accounts and omitted request fields. It is set only through an explicit owner mandate/provisioning request. Saving policy increments the mandate version, disarms execution and requires reconciliation before activation. The existing 100-THB trial explicitly reduces full-policy authority back to a fixed ceiling. Old server rollback ignores the new field and enforces fixed limits. No schema migration or dependency was needed.

That button sends one mandate request; rendering, pairing and policy saving do not arm or reconcile automatically. Normal equity ticks do not invalidate already displayed full-policy consent, while policy changes, binding, Stop, authority and version changes still do.
