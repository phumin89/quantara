# Persistent price plans and verified position slots

The company previously produced allocation actions and repeated HOLD reasoning. A BUY was sized and submitted immediately after a meeting; there was no durable entry/exit plan. A legacy BTC-only account also retained one position slot after its execution allowlist expanded.

## Result

- Live BUY requires an explicit numeric entry ceiling, requested budget-equivalent base quantity, take-profit level, stop level and ISO deadline. Other actions use a null plan; historical paper outputs remain readable. Native price/base steps, inclusive cash fees, wire quote rounding, minimum exit size, capital, cash and order limits are checked. Completed 20-bar low/high and ATR14 are supplied alongside existing forecasts and walk-forward evidence. These levels do not establish profitability.
- Meetings can register multiple plans with distinct identities. Plans are immutable account audit records in the existing PostgreSQL ledger, rather than reconstructed from report prose. Registration validates current authority and aggregate waiting commitments, including room for both the buy and planned sell within the unchanged daily turnover limit. It places no order.
- A separate executor monitor checks every 10 seconds, independently of meeting/AI cadence. It waits for an ask at or below the entry ceiling, rejects an entry after its stop condition breaks, and persists that invalidation so a rebound or restart cannot revive it. Filled entries trigger an exit at target, stop or deadline. The exit uses acquired coins, rounded to the native sell step; imported opening inventory is excluded. Target exits retain the target as their price floor, while other exits use the existing owner slippage bound.
- Plan binding and trigger conditions are rechecked inside durable reservation and dispatch. Existing RiskEngine, account/credential binding, fresh quotes, exact account lease, reconciliation, receipts and exactly-once intent dispatch remain the final controls. An uncertain result is not retried. An expired or invalidated open entry cancels only its saved owned order once, retaining reservations until reconciliation; existing cancellation rules disarm the account.
- Explicit funded owner activation raises saved slots to `max(saved slots, verified market count)` in the same versioned mandate update as the funded order ceiling. A three-market profile therefore replaces the legacy one-slot restriction. The 100-THB trial and ordinary startup preserve saved slots. Pending orders block this change; Stop, allocation, loss/day limits, opening inventory and performance baselines are preserved.
- Trading shows actual persisted plans and actual acquired/sold quantities. Legacy accounts without plan fields remain readable. Settings use a closed disclosure. Chat distinguishes plan registration from broker submission and shows concise reasons when a plan is rejected. A version marker requests the new planning policy at the next eligible meeting; historical reports and completed model work are not rewritten.

## Owner handoff and remaining evidence

Open **Enable Auto Trading.cmd** once to apply the prepared financial services and explicitly upgrade the saved position slots. The regular **Quantara.cmd** remains the normal launcher and preserves financial authority. Owner activation and successful current reconciliation remain required by the repository execution rules. No new manual buy/sell test is required by this change.

The new policy takes effect at the next eligible company meeting, at most once per Bangkok hour. A conditional entry below the current ask waits; a plan is not an immediate buy. Existing verified execution markets remain BTC-THB, ETH-THB and SOL-THB. Missing market history can still exclude a candidate; adding slots does not manufacture evidence or force purchases.

Synthetic checks prove the software behavior, not an actual Bitkub fill, recovery result or profit. Tiny partial fills can fall below the market's sell minimum and are shown for review; closed sell legs can leave sub-step dust, visible in actual account quantities. Rejected, partial or uncertain exits need reconciliation/review, not blind resubmission. Stops are conditional limit-order triggers, not guaranteed fill prices. The initial manual 30-THB purchase remains opening inventory, not a performance result.
