# Multi-asset company review — 2026-10-06

The operating slice expands research from BTC alone to BTC/THB, ETH/THB and SOL/THB. Research permission never grants execution permission. Candidates include their current public bid/ask, their completed daily close, actual held quantities and whether the owner's mandate allows execution. Daily full research uses eight roles; material portfolio reviews use portfolio proposal, risk critique and final proposal. New Live summaries are requested in Thai.

## Review and preserved boundaries

- The owner launcher verifies current market metadata and explicitly activates the verified universe. The preparation command has no ledger writes, order placement or running hosted service. Existing receipt accounting and BTC sizing cannot change. Profile replacement is atomic, locked and backed up.
- Market expansion preserves every owner risk limit, including maximum open positions. An early implementation increased that count; independent review rejected it and the actual code now preserves it. A pending order or owner Stop blocks expansion. An older profile cannot silently remove previously authorized markets. No opening balance, performance baseline, turnover or loss history is reset.
- Hourly reviews are triggered by changed owned money/quantities or >=1% bid/ask movement from the saved checkpoint. Price movement is a review trigger, not a buy/sell rule. Stable observations consume no model call. Pending reservations block reviews, and a saved daily/hourly attempt cannot resubmit after restart.
- Existing daily journal fields/hash remain readable. Hourly attempts are keyed by account, business date and Bangkok hour. Unfinished attempts, missing/checksum-invalid files and backward clocks fail closed. Saved DB reports remain append-only and idempotent; old and new records coexist. Old daily-only binaries reject hourly journals rather than resetting them, so rollback must retain compatible journal readers.
- Research quotes cannot replace quotes in the reconciled financial snapshot. Expanded financial scope must reconcile before execution. The browser receives report summaries, not internal review checkpoints.
- Office telemetry identifies actual watching/waiting/working state and the research/execution universes. It does not animate invented work. Report selection uses attempt ID so multiple rounds in one day stay distinct. Conclusions precede optional team detail.

## Practical limits

This is a three-market Spot portfolio workflow, with one intent per review and existing owner limits. Research uses completed daily bars plus current quotes, not a newly validated intraday strategy. HOLD remains a valid result. The UI shows the latest 31 rounds; earlier reports remain in the ledger. Real filled-order accounting and durable recovery after a real broker interruption still need observed evidence; deterministic fixtures do not prove profitability or completed Live execution.

## Coin discovery and credibility research — proposed next slice

The current market and security specialists receive numerical evidence, not external documents or news. This release does not include autonomous market-wide discovery or a whitepaper reader. Adding two role names alone would misrepresent its capabilities; reuse these existing roles after a source ingestion path is implemented.

The scout should poll public Bitkub symbols, ticker and depth, filter inactive/frozen markets and compare available liquidity, spread, volatility and costs against the account's scale. Preserve held markets in the shortlist. Save only a small shortlist with numerical reasons and changes since the previous observation. Routine scanning should use deterministic calculations; call the specialist only for a changed shortlist or a material new finding. Discovery can cover more markets than execution, but cannot silently widen the verified owner mandate. Bitkub documents public symbol/ticker/depth endpoints, including an all-tickers response when `sym` is omitted: [REST V3 market data](https://github.com/bitkub/bitkub-official-api-docs/blob/master/rest-v3.md#market-data-read-only).

The credibility researcher should summarize original papers against current protocol documentation, code, disclosures, token issuance/unlocks and documented security incidents. Treat a publisher's claims separately from independent corroboration. For example, Ethereum explicitly states its 2014 whitepaper no longer describes the current protocol, and Solana's paper describes plans that may change: [Ethereum whitepaper](https://ethereum.org/whitepaper/), [Solana whitepaper](https://solana.com/solana-whitepaper.pdf). A technical paper is one source, not proof of coin quality or an expected trading return.

Acceptance for that future slice: bounded public-source retrieval with no broker credentials or private URL access; saved URL, document hash, retrieval time and claim-level citations; cached summaries reused unless content changes; missing, conflicting or stale evidence marked unknown; document instructions treated as untrusted data; no use of later sources to fabricate historical research. The meeting receives a concise, deduplicated brief explaining the opportunity, supporting facts, contradictory evidence and unresolved questions. Conclusions and source links are stored for later inspection before any portfolio decision. This is a research capability, not another financial activation gate.
