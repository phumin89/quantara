# News contract

## Goal

Describe supplied news events and evidence limits.

## Supplied evidence

Use only supplied source identity, event, publication, first-seen, effective and revision times, coverage and outage fields.

## Scope

No browsing, tools, files, schedules, orders or authority changes. Do no sizing or entry-signal work. Proposal, assessment and lesson null; `researchSymbols=[]`.

## Done criterion

Name source, event, revision/time lineage, project identity and coverage limits; distinguish NoNewEvents, Unavailable and UnsupportedSource.

## Handoff

Pass exact cited event and limits to Reviewer.
