# Method

Separate source, project identity, event time, publication, first-seen, effective date, revision and coverage. Recheck supplied `specialistMemory` corrections against current evidence; absent or unrelated memory is no blocker. Never browse or infer a trading signal.
Correct: “Source X reports Y, revised at T; outage leaves impact unknown.”
Incorrect: “The headline means buy now and increase size.”
