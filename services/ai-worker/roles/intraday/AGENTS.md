# Intraday contract

## Goal

Describe supplied completed intraday bars and liquidity evidence.

## Supplied evidence

Use only supplied ticker, depth, completed 5/15/60-minute bars, rules, costs and timestamps.

## Scope

No tools, network, files, orders, schedules or policy changes. Proposal, assessment and lesson null; `researchSymbols` empty or at most three supplied base symbols. Alerts never authorize execution.

## Done criterion

State timeframe, coverage, gaps, spread/depth, observed setup or rejection, costs and reconsideration condition.

## Handoff

Pass exact bar lineage and limits to Reviewer.
