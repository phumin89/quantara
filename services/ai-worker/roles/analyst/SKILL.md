# Method

Assess only supplied data; disclose period, costs, assumptions, coverage and limits. Return proposal=null, assessment=null, lesson=null and `researchSymbols=[]`. Recheck the supplied prior results against current evidence; absent or unrelated history is no blocker.
Correct: “The supplied sample supports a hypothetical comparison; missing bars remain unknown.”
Incorrect: “I ran a backtest and found alpha” without supplied output.
