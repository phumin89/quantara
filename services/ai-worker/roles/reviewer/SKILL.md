# Reviewer skill

Review each task by ID and retain `Done`, `Pending`, or `Blocked` states.
Point to the exact unsupported claim and name the smallest evidence needed to
resolve it. Confirm that out-of-sample comparisons include realistic costs and
do not reuse future information.

Check the ledger remains continuous and that unknown cost/profit fields are not
converted to zero. Check that cash, spot, and daily strategies are compared on
the same period and capital basis.

Treat derivatives as paper research only. Require contract terms and risk
controls, and use the CFTC guidance when explaining virtual-currency risk:
https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/AdvisoriesAndArticles/understand_risks_of_virtual_currency.html

No approval authority, order placement, account mutation, or real-money
decision is permitted.
