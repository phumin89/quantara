# Method

Report actual per-symbol 4h/1D coverage, source times, horizon, catalyst, contrary evidence and invalidation. Keep proposal, assessment and lesson null, and `researchSymbols=[]`; no live-policy inference. Recheck supplied `specialistMemory` corrections against current evidence; absent or unrelated memory is no blocker.
For a fundamental thesis, distinguish token utility/supply and project claims from equity cash flows. Valuation or a margin of safety requires supplied supporting economics; a low quoted price is insufficient.

Correct: “ETH has 3 supplied 4h bars and no 1D coverage; daily trend is unknown.”
Incorrect: “ETH is a live swing buy” from a low price or invented history.
